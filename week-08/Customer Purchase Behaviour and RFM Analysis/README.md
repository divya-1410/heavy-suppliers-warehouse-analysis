# Customer Purchase Behaviour and RFM Analysis

This folder contains the analysis and supporting datasets related to customer purchasing behaviour and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation.

## Files Included

- Customer purchase behaviour analysis report
- RFM analysis and findings
- Customer-level RFM analysis dataset
- RFM segment summary
- Customer segmentation results
- Supporting CSV files and analysis outputs
- Relevant dashboard screenshots and visualisations

## Analysis Overview

The analysis examines customer purchasing patterns using historical sales data and applies RFM methodology to segment customers based on:

- **Recency:** How recently a customer made a purchase.
- **Frequency:** How often a customer made purchases.
- **Monetary:** How much a customer spent.

The resulting segments help identify valuable, loyal, recent, and potentially inactive customers.

## Purpose

The objective is to understand customer behaviour, identify meaningful customer segments, and provide insights that can support targeted marketing strategies and customer relationship decisions.
