# Customer Value, Retention and Churn Indicators

This folder contains the analysis reports and supporting datasets focused on customer value, repeat purchasing behaviour, retention patterns, and potential customer churn indicators.

## Files Included

- Customer value analysis report
- Customer retention analysis report
- Customer-level value and retention dataset
- High-value customer analysis
- Potential at-risk customer analysis
- Supporting CSV files and analysis outputs
- Relevant findings and visualisations

## Analysis Overview

The analysis evaluates customer value and purchasing consistency using historical transaction data.

Key areas include:

- **Customer Lifetime Value:** Evaluating the historical purchase value associated with customers.
- **Repeat Purchase Behaviour:** Identifying customers who have made multiple purchases.
- **Customer Retention:** Examining continued purchasing activity and customer engagement.
- **High-Value Customers:** Identifying customers contributing significant purchase value.
- **Potential Churn Indicators:** Highlighting customers whose purchasing activity suggests they may require attention.

## Key Findings

- Total customers analysed: 500
- Historical customer purchase value: 22,958,987,430
- Average historical customer value: 45,917,974.86
- High-value customers identified: 100
- Repeat customers: 500
- Potential at-risk customers: 134 (26.8%)

## Purpose

The objective is to understand customer value and retention patterns, identify valuable customer groups, and recognise potential risks in customer engagement.

These findings can support better customer relationship management, retention strategies, and data-driven business decisions.

## Note

Potential at-risk customers are identified using historical purchasing patterns and defined purchase-gap indicators. They should not automatically be considered confirmed churned customers.
