# Data and Demand Analysis

## 1. Overview

This folder contains the historical demand analysis outputs prepared as part of Week 09 – Part 1: Demand and Inventory Forecasting.

The analysis focuses on understanding product-level demand behaviour and preparing historical demand information for forecasting.

## 2. Objectives

- Analyse historical product demand.
- Examine weekly demand patterns.
- Understand differences in demand across products.
- Prepare demand profiles to support forecasting.
- Generate structured demand datasets for further analysis.

## 3. Files Included

| File | Description |
|---|---|
| `weekly_product_demand.csv` | Weekly product-level demand information |
| `week9_product_demand_profile.csv` | Product-level demand profile for the analysis |

## 4. Analysis Areas

### Weekly Product Demand
Examination of product demand across weekly periods using historical sales information.

### Product Demand Profiles
Organisation of product-level demand information to support demand analysis and forecasting.
